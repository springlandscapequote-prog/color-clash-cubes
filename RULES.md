# Color Clash Cubes — the rules

*Invented by Gabriela Rubio (2025).*

## What you need
72 `#` pieces: **12 of each of 6 colors** (yellow, teal, lilac, burgundy, blue, red). They start in the **pool**.
One **number die** (1–6) and one **color cube**. 2 to 4 players.

## The goal
Build the most **safe cubes**. 6 pieces of the same color snap together into a **cube**, and a finished cube is **safe 🔒**: nobody can ever steal it. There are 12 cubes in the whole game (2 of each color).

## On your turn
1. **Roll** the number die and the color cube.
   *If the cube lands on a color that is totally built (both cubes done), it rolls again by itself.*
2. **Pick one:**
   * ✋ **TAKE** that many pieces of that color from the pool, or
   * 😈 **STEAL** up to that many pieces of that color from **one** other player's loose pieces.
     You get as many as the die says, or everything they have if they have less.
   * If the pool has *fewer* pieces than the die says, you may take what's left **and** steal the rest from one player.
   * You must do something if you can. No skipping!
3. **Make cubes.** Every time you hold 6 loose pieces of one color, they snap into a cube. It is safe 🔒. Extra pieces stay loose in your pile.
4. **Underdog re-roll 🔄.** If another player has more safe cubes than you, you may roll **both** dice a second time (once per turn).

Only **loose** pieces can be stolen. Safe cubes never can.

## When does it end?
* When **all 12 cubes** are built, **or**
* when the **last piece leaves the pool** it is the **LAST LAP 🏁**: every other player gets **one more turn** (last chance to steal!), then the game ends.

## Who wins?
Most safe cubes. If there's a tie:
1. the player with the most **different colors** of cubes wins, then
2. the most **loose pieces** left, then
3. the player who **built the last cube**.

(In the very rare case that nothing separates two players, they share the win.)
