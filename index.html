How to play

  1. Roll the number die and the color cube.
  2. Take that many pieces of that color from the pool. (If the cube lands on a color that is all built, it rolls again by itself.)
  3. Get 6 of one color and they snap into a cube. Safe cubes are locked 🔒 and can never be stolen!
  4. Or be sneaky and steal pieces of that color from another player's pile. Safe cubes can't be stolen, only loose pieces.
  5. Behind in cubes? You get an Underdog re-roll 🔄.
  6. When the last piece leaves the pool it's the LAST LAP: everyone gets one more turn. The game also ends if all 12 cubes are built.
  7. Most safe cubes wins! Tie? The player with the most colors wins, then the most loose pieces, then whoever built the last cube.
